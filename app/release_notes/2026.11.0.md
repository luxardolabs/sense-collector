# 2026.11.0 — 2026-11-05

Sign-in for accounts with two-factor authentication, a collector that tells you when it can't connect, and a security update.

The labels after each item are this collector's surfaces: `Collector` (the running service) and `Docs`.

## NEW

- **Two-factor accounts can sign in** `Collector` — If your Sense account has two-factor authentication turned on, set SENSE_COLLECTOR_MFA_SECRET to the account's authenticator secret and the collector signs in on its own; without it, it now stops with a message naming that setting.

## IMPROVED

- **A collector that can't connect now restarts instead of going quiet** `Collector` — After six rejected realtime connections in a row it exits so your restart policy brings it back and you can see the problem, rather than running with no data.
- **Each prod stack keeps its own output folder** `Collector` — The two prod stacks now read OUTPUT_DIR_TYLE and OUTPUT_DIR_BB; if you set OUTPUT_DIR for a prod stack, switch to the new name before upgrading.
- **Your stacks survive a full machine** `Collector` — When the machine runs out of memory, test and maintenance jobs are stopped before your collectors and databases.
- **Clearer setup docs** `Docs` — Two-factor setup, the connection limit and the per-stack folders are now documented.

## FIXED

- **Security update** `Collector` — The image carries the latest operating-system security fixes, and the collector refuses to talk to the Sense cloud without encryption and keeps your sign-in token out of its logs.
- **Data from unusual readings** `Collector` — A reading with a malformed time is skipped instead of being lost with the rest of its batch or stored at the wrong date, and device-change events keep their timestamp.
- **Clean shutdown** `Collector` — Stopping the collector waits for all of its background work to finish.
- **Reliability** `Collector` — Lots of behind-the-scenes stability work.
