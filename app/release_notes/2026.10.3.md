# 2026.10.3 — 2026-10-25

A security and reliability release from a full review of the collector: your credentials stay out of the logs, your data stops going missing in odd cases, and the health check tells the truth. Check the two "before you upgrade" items first.

Sense Collector is headless; the labels below name its two surfaces: `Collector` (the running collector) and `Deployment` (the image and the stacks you run).

## Before you upgrade

- **Set the image version** `Deployment` — Your stack's env file must name the release it runs (TAG=2026.10.3); the stack now refuses to start with it empty.
- **Use your own InfluxDB token** `Collector` — If your stack reads your real Sense account with one of the example tokens shipped in this repository, the collector now stops at startup and says why: give your InfluxDB its own token.

## NEW

- **Safer bundled Grafana** `Deployment` — The bundled dashboards listen only on this machine unless you choose otherwise, sign in to InfluxDB with a read-only account, and their data source can no longer be changed from the web page. Set a Grafana admin password and a read-only InfluxDB password in your env file before your next start.
- **Startup refuses unsafe settings** `Collector` — The collector stops with a clear reason instead of running with a password inside a server address, or with an export folder it cannot write.

## IMPROVED

- **Gentler on your Sense account** `Collector` — During a Sense sign-in outage or rate limit the collector backs off instead of retrying every second, and waits as long as Sense asks.
- **Settings out of range are adjusted, not ignored** `Collector` — A number above or below a setting's limit now uses the nearest allowed value (and says so), instead of silently falling back to the default.
- **Faster, cleaner shutdown** `Collector` — Stopping the collector on a dead connection takes about two seconds instead of being cut off mid-save.
- **Private exports** `Collector` — If you turn on raw-data exports, the files are readable only by the collector's user, and each device gets its own file name.
- **Monitors without voltage sensing** `Collector` — A Sense monitor that reports power but no line frequency is now recorded and counted as healthy.
- **Security updates** `Deployment` — The image's web libraries are updated past four published vulnerabilities (one critical).

## FIXED

- **Passwords and tokens in logs** `Collector` — Startup no longer prints part of your password or token, and debug logging no longer leaks your Sense sign-in token.
- **Readings that stopped for days** `Collector` — A reading Sense sent as a whole number could block whole-house power from being saved for up to a week; readings are now always saved.
- **One odd value lost a whole update** `Collector` — A single malformed device value no longer throws away the rest of that second's readings.
- **A stuck collector** `Collector` — Always-on devices without a name could freeze the collector entirely; they are now skipped.
- **Healthy while doing nothing** `Collector` — The health check now goes unhealthy when no usable power readings arrive, including when your Sense monitor is offline.
- **Device events in the same second** `Collector` — A device switching on and off within one second is now recorded as two events, not one.
- **Stale device details** `Collector` — Device details are no longer re-saved with an old snapshot stamped as new.
- **Device names ending in a backslash** `Collector` — These are now stored as the name you see in the Sense app.
- **Events lost on stop** `Collector` — Device events still waiting for a name are saved when the collector stops.
- **Always-on yearly cost** `Collector` — The always-on yearly cost is now in the same unit as every other device's (it was 100 times too large); the first week after upgrading may show a gap in that one value.
- **Dashboards pointing nowhere** `Deployment` — Each bundled Grafana now reads its own stack's data.
