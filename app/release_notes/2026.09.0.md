# 2026.09.0 — 2026-09-06

Security patches and a health-check fix. Drop-in upgrade — nothing to reconfigure.

## FIXED

- **Security updates in bundled libraries** `Collector` — Four known vulnerabilities were patched in the libraries this collector uses to talk to InfluxDB and to Sense. Upgrading is recommended.
- **Health status could be wrong for an hour after a daylight-saving change** `Collector` — Around a clock change, a healthy collector could be reported as unhealthy (or a stuck one as healthy), which could restart a container that was working fine. Health is now measured against a clock that does not shift.
- **Log timestamps around reconnects and startup** `Collector` — These could read an hour off after a clock change. They now always match your container's time.
- **Startup warnings ignored your log-level settings** `Collector` — Warnings about your configuration were printed outside your chosen log levels, so they could not be filtered or quieted like everything else. They now follow the same settings as the rest of the logs.

## CHANGED

- **Pre-2026 releases have been removed from this repository** `Deployment` — The four old `v1.x`/`v2.x` releases and their tags are gone. They came from before this project moved to Luxardo Labs, and their files referenced internal infrastructure that should not have been public. If you are still running one of those versions, move to the current image — those tags will no longer resolve.

## IMPROVED

- **Real faults are no longer hidden as routine hiccups** `Collector` — The collector still keeps running when an individual reading or write fails, exactly as before. What changed is that a genuine internal fault now shows up in your logs instead of being recorded as an ordinary transient failure and passed over.
- **Test and demo stacks stop leaving disk behind** `Deployment` — Running the built-in demo or end-to-end test repeatedly used to leave an orphaned data volume behind each time. It no longer does.
- **Reliability** `Collector` — Substantial behind-the-scenes work on test coverage and automated checks. Nothing to do; the collector should simply be steadier.

______________________________________________________________________

Upgrading: pull the new image and restart. No changes to your environment file, your compose file, your InfluxDB buckets or your Grafana dashboards, and nothing to migrate.

Surfaces used above: `Collector` (the running collector — what it reads, writes and logs) and `Deployment` (the image and the stacks you run to operate it).

Still not supported: Sense accounts with multi-factor authentication enabled. Tracked as issue [#8](https://github.com/luxardolabs/sense-collector/issues/8).
